Frequently asked questions

Answers before you reach out.

A few practical details about how support works, the businesses I serve, and what to expect next.

What accounting support do you provide?

Support includes QuickBooks bookkeeping, accounting cleanup, financial analysis, US tax preparation support, and accounting workflows for service and ecommerce businesses.

Do you work with remote and international clients?

Yes. I am based in the Philippines and provide remote support for clients in the United States and other international markets.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

The primary focus is on service and ecommerce businesses that need dependable books, useful reporting, and a clearer view of cash flow and profitability.

Which accounting tools do you use?

QuickBooks Online is the primary focus, with experience across Xero, A2X, Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and Hubdoc.

How do we get started?

Send a short note describing your business, the accounting tools you use, and the support you need. From there, we can identify the most useful next step.
