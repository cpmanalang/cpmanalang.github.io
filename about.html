About Manalang Accounting

Accounting that gives business owners room to focus.

With more than eight years of experience in accounting and auditing, I have supported over 50 clients with bookkeeping, accounting cleanup, reconciliations, financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, audit support, and analysis.

My primary expertise is QuickBooks and practical accounting for US service and ecommerce businesses. I work carefully, communicate proactively, and treat financial information with the confidentiality it deserves.
